Our Mission

HealthXHer is an inclusive, interdisciplinary hackathon that brings together students from a wide range of academic backgrounds, including life sciences, healthcare, economics, policy and management, data science, and AI. By uniting diverse perspectives and skill sets, the event aims to address the gender health gap in clinical research and tackle challenges that disproportionately affect women in healthcare.

The hackathon connects talented students from across Sweden with AstraZeneca, supporting their professional growth as early talent while increasing awareness of career opportunities within the life sciences industry.

Beyond innovation, HealthXHer seeks to raise awareness of the vital role the life sciences play in society. Through mentorship, collaboration, and hands-on problem-solving, participants are empowered to co-create impactful, real-world solutions and contribute to a more equitable healthcare future.

About HealthXHer

An interdisciplinary hackathon dedicated to closing the gender health gap and shaping the future of women’s health.

Hackathon Objective

HealthXHer is dedicated to fostering an inclusive and diverse environment, welcoming participants from a wide spectrum of academic and professional backgrounds. These include Life Sciences, Healthcare, Economics, Policy and Management, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and beyond.

Our vision is to unite a rich mix of perspectives and expertise around a shared mission: addressing and reducing the gender health gap in clinical trials and tackling healthcare challenges uniquely faced by women.

The event connects talented students from across Sweden with AstraZeneca, supporting their professional development as early talent and increasing awareness of career opportunities within the company.

The HealthXHer Experience

HealthXHer is designed to be more than a hackathon. Participants benefit from a multifaceted experience that combines mentorship, collaboration, and professional exposure.

Throughout the event, participants gain access to experienced mentors, expand their professional networks, and collaborate with peers from diverse disciplines. This collaborative environment is key to co-creating innovative, impactful solutions.

A core part of our mission is also to raise public awareness of the life sciences industry, highlighting its importance and societal impact.

What Participants Gain

  • Showcase your talent to a leading organization such as AstraZeneca
  • Learn from professionals working in Clinical Trials, AI, Life Sciences, LLMs, HR, and Recruitment
  • Expand your professional and academic network
  • Participate in workshops on clinical trials, career development, AI, and women’s health
  • Apply your skills in a truly interdisciplinary setting
  • Contribute to the discussion around the gender health gap and raise awareness

Mentorship Structure

Throughout the hackathon, participants have access to both general and specialized mentors.

General mentors are available to all teams via Slack to answer quick questions and provide guidance.

Specialized mentors are assigned to finalist teams in the week leading up to the finals, offering deeper technical, strategic, and presentation support.

Mentors bring expertise across healthcare, AI, digital health, public policy, design thinking, entrepreneurship, and communication.

Teams & Submissions

Team placement is first-come, first-served. For each participating university, the first five teams submitting a complete and correct application will be accepted.

Submission guidelines are detailed in the Participants Guide, which is accessible exclusively to registered participants.

Intellectual Property

Participants retain full ownership of their background knowledge and all intellectual property developed during HealthXHer. HealthXHer does not claim ownership over any participant-developed projects.

Media Recording & Usage

Photos, video, and audio recordings may be taken during the event. By participating, you consent to the use of these materials for follow-up activities and future promotional purposes. Participants may contact the organizers in advance with any concerns.

Winners will be announced during the National Finals on 13th March 2026.

The Challenge Tracks

Below are the three overarching challenge tracks for this year’s Hackathon, designed by our Campus Ambassadors. For detailed context, objectives, and judging criteria for each track, please read The Participation Guide

Oncology safety: early detection of sex-specific adverse events

Did you know women undergoing oncology treatments face a staggering 34% higher risk of adverse events (AEs) compared to men? From debilitating nausea and fatigue to critical hematologic issues like anemia, these sex-specific toxicities can significantly impact treatment effectiveness and quality of life. Biological factors, drug pharmacokinetics, and immune responses all play a role, creating an urgent need for tailored solutions.

  • Your Mission: We're calling on innovators to design groundbreaking solutions that advance the detection, monitoring, and management of oncology adverse events, with deep respect and consideration for sex-specific differences.

Innovating in Cancer Screening and Early Detection for Women

One million women die each year from breast, endometrial, and ovarian cancer—many deaths are preventable with early detection. Yet routine screenings are often missed due to fear of results, low awareness, misinformation, anxiety, procedural discomfort, cultural barriers, and limited access. These obstacles are magnified by socioeconomic status, age, and geography, creating stark disparities and leaving under‑screened groups behind.

  • Your mission: Design bold, practical strategies that boost screening and early detection by removing barriers and personalizing care. Focus on a clear step in the pathway—outreach, risk personalisation, or access enablement—and deliver end‑to‑end impact.

Improving Autoimmune Disease Diagnosis and Management in Women

Women account for up to 80% of autoimmune disease cases, yet diagnosis is slow and fragmented. Symptoms—fatigue, joint/muscle pain, brain fog—overlap with other conditions, and specific tests are limited, leading to an average 4-year diagnostic delay and multiple consultations. Care is often split across specialties, with under-recognition of fatigue, pain, and mental health impacts on quality of life.

  • Your mission: Develop solutions that streamline diagnosis and management for women with autoimmune diseases. Aim for scalable, equity-focused concepts (low-tech or digital) that improve recognition, triage, and follow-up, and reduce the burden of symptoms on daily life.

Why should I join?

Participating in HealthXHer offers a unique opportunity to collaborate with diverse talents, gain hands-on experience in addressing real-world female health challenges, and showcase your skills to industry leaders. You'll have access to mentorship from experts, networking opportunities, and the chance to win exciting prizes that can further your career. Most importantly, you'll contribute to meaningful solutions that can positively impact female health outcomes. Check out our Prizes and Mentors & Jury sections for more details!

Mandatory Team Size

All teams must consist of exactly 4 participants. We require teams to be multidisciplinary to ensure robust solutions for the complex tracks.

Selection Criteria

Teams are accepted on a first-come, first-served basis. You are expected to register individually, however there is no restriction on forming teams with friends or colleagues, make sure all members register separately in order to secure your team. If you register without a team, or half a team, we'll match you into teams on Slack.

How are the teams formed and matched?

Post-kickoff, everyone will be required to post a short bio on Slack. Those who already have a team can create a private channel for their team, whilst we will help match individuals without a team into groups of 4 based on skills and interests.

In-Person Commitment

The kick off and the first project weeks will be held online, teams are free to meet up in person to work on their projects. However, the Semi-Finals (Lund/Gothenburg/Stockholm) and the Grand Final (AZ Gothenburg) are mandatory physical events. Travel assistance will be provided to finalists.

What universities are involved in HealthXHer

We are collaborating with top universities across Sweden including Lund University, Gothenburg University, Chalmers University of Technology, Stockholm University, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, and Karolinska Institute.

Will there be mentors or professionals for guidance?

Yes, throughout the hackathon, teams will have access to a panel of mentors and industry experts from AstraZeneca and partner organizations. They will provide guidance, feedback, and support to help teams refine their ideas and solutions.

What is the final deliverable?

The final deliverable will be a working prototype or solution that teams have developed during the hackathon. This could include a functional app, a detailed design document, or a comprehensive report outlining their approach and findings. However, teams do not need fully working prototype, concept-stage solutions are also acceptable as long as they effectively address the challenge.

Intellectual Property and Data Privacy

All intellectual property rights in the deliverables created during the hackathon will remain with the respective teams. However, teams are encouraged to share their work openly and contribute to the broader community. Data privacy will be handled in accordance with GDPR and other applicable data protection laws, all data will be securely deleted within 30 days after the finals on 13th March. Participants may access, correct, or request deletion of their data during the event, and all information is stored on secure, access-controlled platforms and never used for purposes beyond administering the Hackathon.
